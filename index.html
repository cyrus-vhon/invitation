Good to Know

Due to our intimate venue capacity, we can only accommodate guests listed on our invitation. We truly appreciate your understanding and look forward to celebrating with you.

We have planned an adults-only celebration so that all our guests can fully relax and enjoy the evening. We hope you understand and appreciate your support.

Yes, complimentary parking is available at both venues. Our ushers will be happy to guide you upon arrival.

Help us bring our dream of a formal wedding ceremony to life. Gentlemen: strictly Barong Tagalog paired with black slacks. Ladies: strictly long, floor-length dresses or gowns. We gently request no t-shirts, polo shirts, shorts, slippers, female pants, mini skirts, or cocktail gowns.

We kindly ask that you arrive at least 30 minutes before the ceremony begins to allow time for seating and to avoid any disruptions during the processional.

We welcome you to take as many photos and videos as you'd like during our ceremony and reception! Please help us preserve these memories by scanning the QR code provided to upload your captures to our digital wedding album. We just kindly ask that you refrain from blocking or stepping into the center aisle while the entourage and bride are walking, so our photo and video team have a clear, unobstructed view to capture these once-in-a-lifetime moments perfectly.
